### Headline Code & Setup Findings

1. **GNN `QW` weights negative quirk-to-pileup edges equally with positive pair edges ([`train_gnn.py:L65`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/gnn/train_gnn.py#L65), [`train_filter.py:L65`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/gnn/train_filter.py#L65))**:
   - `w = w * np.where(hq[src] | hq[dst], QW, 1.0)` with `pos_weight=1.0` scales **both** true same-pair edges (`hq[s] & hq[d]`, $y=1$) and wrong quirk-to-pileup edges (`hq[s] ^ hq[d]`, $y=0$) by `QW`.
   - From $\mu=0 \to \mu=60$, true pair edges per event stay constant ($\sim 80\text{--}160$) while wrong quirk-touching edges grow $\sim 16\times$ ($\sim 1,000\text{--}1,500$ in `v3_mu0f_cut0.1`, and $\sim 14,000$ in the unfiltered 22.8M-edge graph).
   - Consequently, even the running `QW=40` GNN arm leaves the ~1:15 positive-to-negative imbalance *among quirk-touching edges* untouched, shifting uncertain pair-edge logits down by $\sim -\ln(15) \approx -2.7$ (explaining `AUC = 0.953` vs recall `0.424` at cut `0.5`).
   - In [`train_filter_stream.py:L104`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/pileup60/train_filter_stream.py#L104), keeping all `hq[s] | hq[t]` edges with `QW=50` created a ~250:1 weighted negative gradient on quirk hits, explaining why fine-tuning the edge filter at $\mu=60$ degraded quirk recall (`0.76` vs `0.82` at 1.1M edges).

2. **Global unweighted CC loses 63% of the GNN's tight-match ceiling ([`build_tracks.py:L128-136`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/gnn/build_tracks.py#L128-L136), [EXPERIMENTS.md:L1129-1133](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/EXPERIMENTS.md#L1129-L1133))**:
   - At cut $0.5$, true pair edges $>0.5$ (`ceiling (ii)`) already reach **`0.569 / 0.247`** (loose / tight), whereas built tracks get **`0.535 / 0.090`**. A single wrong bridge edge $>0.5$ merges an entire 10–15 hit helical pileup track into the 26-hit pair, ruining tight ($>90\%$) and loose ($>50\%$) purity.

3. **Unnormalized sum aggregation & `phi_crop` inefficiencies ([`common.py:L203-206`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/gnn/common.py#L203-L206), [`train_gnn60.py:L60-73`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/pileup60/train_gnn60.py#L60-L73))**:
   - [`InteractionGNN`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/gnn/common.py#L186-L216) uses raw `index_add_` sum without edge gating (`sigmoid(gate(e)) * e`), so ~30–60 incident pileup edges per quirk hit drown out the 2–4 true pair edges during message passing.
   - [`phi_crop`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/pileup60/train_gnn60.py#L43-L57) runs *after* [`T.batch`](file:///global/cfs/cdirs/m4958/usr/danieltm/ColliderML/quirkml/tracking/gnn/train_gnn.py#L51-L68) computes features on all 1.49M edges, never compacts the ~70k node tensor `f` (wasting ~70% of node MLP compute on degree-0 hits), and centers on a single random quirk hit only 50% of the time (`quirk_frac=0.5`), frequently slicing wide pairs in half.
